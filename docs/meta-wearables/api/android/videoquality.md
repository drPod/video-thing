# VideoQuality Enum

Defines the quality levels available for video streaming from Meta Wearables devices. Higher quality levels provide better resolution but may require more bandwidth and processing power.

## See Also

-   [StreamConfiguration](/docs/reference/android/dat/0.6/com_meta_wearable_dat_camera_types_streamconfiguration)

## Signature

enum VideoQuality : Enum<VideoQuality\> 

## Enumeration Constants

Member

Description

HIGH

High quality video streaming (720x1280 resolution)

MEDIUM

Medium quality video streaming (504x896 resolution)

LOW

Low quality video streaming (360x640 resolution)
